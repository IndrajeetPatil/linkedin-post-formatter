Privacy

The formatter is free and private: what you write stays on your device.

Free

No price, no adverts, no account, and no sign-up. The formatter is open-source software under the MIT licence, so anyone can read its code.

Your post stays with you

Your post is converted inside this browser, by the md2linkedin Python package running on WebAssembly. The text is never uploaded: there is no server that could receive it. Copying puts it on your clipboard and nowhere else.

Saved on this device

Your draft, the colour scheme, and the two output options are saved in this browser, on this device, so they are there when you come back. Clear post empties the draft, and clearing your browser data for the site deletes all of it.

What the page downloads

GitHub Pages serves the page, its fonts, and its Python packages. The Python runtime, Pyodide, comes from the jsDelivr network. Like any download, these requests show GitHub and jsDelivr your IP address and browser, but never your text.

Counting visits

Like the rest of indrapatil.com, the site counts visits with Google Analytics: Google learns that the page was opened, with details such as your browser and rough location, but never your text. The privacy notice has the details. The formatter works the same if your browser blocks it.
